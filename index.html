Noir · Unity

Project Dunwell

Designed and shipped a neo-noir locked-room mystery with three branching endings, nine discoverable clues, and a custom InkBridge architecture that keeps Unity and Ink fully decoupled — Unity owns world logic and collision triggers, Ink owns all narrative output, with tag-driven events coordinating UI, scene transitions, and story state without either system reaching into the other.
